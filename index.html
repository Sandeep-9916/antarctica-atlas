STRATUM 1 // SURFACE & DRY VALLEYS (0m – 99m)

Surface Stations, McMurdo Dry Valleys & The 21-km VLF Antenna

At the surface, Antarctica's East Antarctic Plateau reaches -89.2°C at Vostok Station, while the ice-free McMurdo Dry Valleys receive only 10 mm of water-equivalent precipitation per year (mean temperature -19.8°C). Here, researchers also built Siple Station's massive 21-kilometer-long dipole antenna directly on the snow to transmit very-low-frequency (VLF) radio signals through Earth's magnetosphere to Roberval, Quebec.
